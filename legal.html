Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information GreenFeast ("we", "us", "our") collects through the mobile app and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account details: name, phone number, email (if you sign in with Google, Apple, or email).
  • Delivery information: addresses, landmarks, and map pins you provide.
  • Health & dietary information: height, weight, goals, allergens, and dietary preferences you enter during onboarding, used solely to personalise your meal recommendations.
  • Order & payment information: subscription plan, order history, wallet balance and transactions. Payments are processed by Cashfree; we do not store your card or UPI credentials.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate the Service: creating your account, building your meal plan and recommendations, scheduling and delivering orders, processing payments, and providing customer support.

3. Sharing

We share information with service providers strictly as needed to run the Service — for example, our payment processor (Cashfree) and our delivery operations team. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account and order data for as long as your account is active, and as needed to comply with legal obligations (such as tax records for payments).

5. Your Choices

You can view and update your profile information from the Account tab. You can request deletion of your account and associated data via the in-app "Delete my account" option or by contacting support; some information may be retained where required by law. See Delete your account for full instructions.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 18.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll reflect the current version's date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to greenfeast.tech@gmail.com or via WhatsApp support.

Delete your account

Applies to the GreenFeast app (com.greenfeast.app)

You can permanently delete your GreenFeast account and its associated data at any time, directly from inside the app. You do not need to contact us to do it.

How to delete your account from the app

  1. Open the GreenFeast app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete my account.
  4. Review the summary of what you'll lose, then type DELETE to confirm.
  5. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

This cannot be undone. Deleting your account permanently cancels any active subscription, including deliveries you have remaining, and any balance left in your in-app wallet is forfeited and cannot be refunded. There is no way for us to recover a deleted account.

What data is deleted

When you delete your account, the following are permanently erased from our systems:

  • Your login and profile — name, phone number, email address, and authentication record
  • Dietary profile and onboarding questionnaire responses (height, weight, goals, allergens, preferences)
  • Saved delivery addresses and map locations
  • Your subscription and its delivery schedule
  • Order history and any scheduled upcoming orders
  • Wallet, wallet balance, and wallet transaction history
  • Payment records associated with your account
  • Notifications and your push notification token

What we may retain, and for how long

Where Indian law requires it — principally financial and tax records relating to payments you have made — we may retain a minimal transaction record for the statutory retention period. These records are kept solely for legal and accounting compliance, are not used to contact you or to build a profile, and are not shared for marketing purposes.

If you can't access the app

If you're unable to sign in and would like your account deleted, contact us and we'll process the request on your behalf:

  • Email greenfeast.tech@gmail.com from the address registered to your account, or
  • Message us on WhatsApp from your registered phone number

Please include the name and phone number or email on the account so we can verify it's yours. We aim to action verified deletion requests within 7 working days.
